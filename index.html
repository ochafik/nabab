Interactions

Hover a barWhat-if preview: the horizontal position picks 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100% for that outcome; the whole network shows the resulting change (green gain, red loss, halo = size of change). Unaffected nodes dim.
Click a barCommit exactly the previewed value (100% = hard evidence, otherwise soft evidence via Jeffrey's rule, 0% rules the outcome out)
Drag a thumbSet a continuous soft-evidence value; click a thumb to clear it
Click a nameCycle hard evidence through the outcomes, then clear
Eye icon / SpaceToggle observations on the node / the selected nodes
Alt-click a bar, or right-click a nodeIntervene: do(X = outcome). Incoming edges are cut (dashed); repeat to clear. Previews and inference use the mutilated network.
ExplainMark the most probable explanation of the evidence on every node; list the top 5 by log-probability
Click a node, Shift-clickSelect (value of information, CPT, sensitivity panels); extend the selection
Scroll / ⌘orCtrl+scroll / pinchPan / zoom
